## Hi, I'm Tiziano

I'm **Tiziano Tomas Luzi Ramos**, a developer and open-source enthusiast based in **Balcarce, Argentina**. I work on backend and web applications, with a particular interest in systems programming, reverse engineering, and understanding how things work beneath the interface.

I like building useful tools, exploring unfamiliar technology, and learning by making things from scratch. My projects range from API tooling to Windows filesystems, with interests extending into Android security, embedded systems, and simulators.

[Explore my portfolio](https://portfolio.bid/) or [browse my code on GitHub](https://github.com/tizianoluziramos).

## What I work on

- **Backend development:** Node.js, TypeScript, APIs, and tools that make developing and documenting applications easier.
- **Systems and low-level software:** C++, filesystems, custom operating-system experiments, and terminal-based projects.
- **Security and reverse engineering:** Android, firmware, embedded systems, and secure development.
- **Simulation and experimentation:** exploring how phones, PCs, and other systems behave by building and studying them.

## My toolbox

TypeScript and Python are among my favorite languages. I also work with JavaScript, Node.js, C++, Bash, HTML, and CSS, and recent projects have taken me into Go.

My tools include Git, Linux, Docker, and VS Code. On the database side, my portfolio lists MySQL, PostgreSQL, and MariaDB, alongside Scrum for organizing work.

I keep learning across both application development and lower-level topics. My [portfolio](https://portfolio.bid/) collects courses and certificates covering full-stack development, Node.js, C#, Postman, ethical hacking, and Scrum.

## Why this blog exists

This is where I document what I'm building: how projects work, how to get them running, and the details worth keeping somewhere other than a commit message.

The portfolio is the overview, GitHub is the code, and this blog is the space for the explanations in between.

Outside programming, I'm interested in gaming and new technology. I also enjoy open source and the opportunity to learn from other people's work.
